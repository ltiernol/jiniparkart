Jini Park

Multidisciplinary Artist — Los Angeles, CA

Jini Park is a multidisciplinary artist working primarily in painting, screen printing, and mixed media. As a first-generation Korean immigrant, her practice explores cultural inheritance, identity, and human existence beyond societal constraints. She merges traditional Korean techniques with contemporary Western methodologies to examine the tension between tradition and modernity, seeking an identity that is fluid, evolving, and unrestricted. Her work aims to liberate herself and others from the burdens of the past while honoring the histories that have shaped them.
